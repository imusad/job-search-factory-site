Job Search Factory

Job Search Factory is a private, single-user tool that helps its owner run a personal job search. Twice a day it collects public job listings from company career pages and job boards, scores them, and records them in the owner's own Google Sheet. When the owner approves a listing, it drafts application documents into the owner's own Google Drive.

The app is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

It uses Google sign-in to access the owner's Google Sheets, Google Drive (only files the app creates), Google Docs, Google Slides and Gmail (send only), so it can maintain the tracker, create documents and email the owner a daily summary.
